# shared-browser

A browser that a person and an AI use together. The browser keeps running as one place, and each comes to it from outside: the person through a screen, the AI through CDP. They see the same tabs, use the same logged-in state, and whatever one of them does is visible to the other. There is no rule that the person leads and the AI follows, or the reverse. What a person does, such as logging in, passing two-factor checks, or paying, and what an AI does, such as researching or filling in forms, alternate within one session.

Where the person, the AI, and the browser are is not fixed. All three may sit on one machine or in three different countries, and the way to reach the browser is the same. There may be more than one person and more than one AI. The browser keeps its logins and history after a conversation or a task ends, so the next visit starts where the last one left off.
